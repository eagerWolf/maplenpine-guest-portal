# Hospitality OS

## Product Roadmap

Version: 1.0
Status: Draft
Audience: Executive Team, Product Management, Engineering, Investors, Partners

---

# 1. Purpose

The Product Roadmap describes the long-term evolution of Hospitality OS.

Unlike implementation plans or sprint backlogs, this document defines the strategic direction of the platform over multiple years.

The roadmap provides a shared vision for engineering, product management, partners and investors.

---

# 2. Vision

Hospitality OS is designed to become the operating system for modern hospitality.

The platform will evolve from a guest experience platform into a complete operational ecosystem connecting:

* Guests
* Property Owners
* Property Managers
* Staff
* Local Service Providers
* Smart Properties
* Artificial Intelligence

Every interaction should occur within Hospitality OS.

---

# 3. Strategic Phases

The evolution of Hospitality OS is divided into multiple strategic phases.

Each phase builds upon the previous one while preserving architectural stability.

---

# Phase 1 — Core Platform

Objective:

Build a stable, modular and extensible platform foundation.

Primary capabilities include:

* Reservation Management
* Guest Portal
* Digital Check-In
* Smart Access
* Messaging
* Property Guide
* Payments
* Workflow Engine
* Provider Framework
* Administration Backoffice

Success Criteria

* Production-ready platform
* Stable API
* Modular architecture
* Multi-property support

---

# Phase 2 — Marketplace

Objective:

Transform Hospitality OS into a digital marketplace.

Marketplace services include:

* Breakfast
* Bike Rental
* Luggage Storage
* Airport Transfers
* Wellness
* Local Experiences
* Restaurants
* Equipment Rental

Future marketplace categories may be added without changing the platform architecture.

Success Criteria

* Active service providers
* Marketplace revenue
* Digital ordering workflows
* Automated fulfillment

---

# Phase 3 — Smart Property

Objective:

Digitally connect physical hospitality properties.

Capabilities include:

* Smart Locks
* Climate Control
* Lighting
* Energy Management
* Water Monitoring
* Sensors
* EV Charging
* Parking Access
* Building Automation

Success Criteria

* Fully automated guest arrival
* Energy optimization
* Device monitoring
* Workflow-driven automation

---

# Phase 4 — AI Platform

Objective:

Make Artificial Intelligence a native platform capability.

AI responsibilities include:

* Guest Assistant
* Staff Assistant
* Operational Assistant
* Workflow Automation
* Knowledge Retrieval
* Recommendations
* Reporting
* Analytics

AI should execute platform capabilities through documented APIs rather than bypassing business logic.

Success Criteria

* High AI resolution rate
* Reduced operational workload
* AI-assisted workflows
* Context-aware guest support

---

* Provider Independence

---

# 8. Long-Term Vision

Hospitality OS is not intended to become just another Property Management System.

The long-term objective is to establish Hospitality OS as the digital operating platform for the hospitality industry.

The platform should unify guest experience, operational management, property automation, artificial intelligence and local service ecosystems into a single extensible architecture.

Through modularity, provider independence and a strong developer ecosystem, Hospitality OS should continuously evolve without requiring fundamental architectural redesign.

Ultimately, Hospitality OS aims to become the trusted digital infrastructure on which modern hospitality businesses operate, innovate and grow.
