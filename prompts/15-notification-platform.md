# Hospitality OS

## Notification Platform

Version: 1.0
Status: Draft
Audience: Software Architects, Backend Developers, Frontend Developers, Product Owners

---

# 1. Purpose

The Notification Platform is responsible for delivering all notifications generated by Hospitality OS.

Rather than allowing each module to send notifications independently, the Notification Platform provides a centralized communication layer responsible for message generation, delivery, scheduling and tracking.

This ensures consistency across the platform while allowing notifications to be delivered through multiple communication channels.

---

# 2. Vision

The Notification Platform should provide a unified communication experience for guests, property managers, staff and service providers.

Every notification should:

* be generated automatically
* use the recipient's preferred language
* use the preferred communication channel
* respect notification preferences
* be fully traceable

Communication should become an integrated part of every business workflow.

---

# 3. Design Principles

## Event Driven

Notifications are triggered by business events.

Examples:

* Reservation Created
* Check-in Completed
* Payment Received
* Marketplace Order Created
* Door PIN Generated

The Notification Platform listens for events and decides what should be delivered.

---

## Channel Independent

Business logic never sends emails or SMS messages directly.

Instead it requests a notification.

The Notification Platform selects the appropriate delivery channel.

---

## Template Based

Every notification is generated from templates.

Templates contain:

* localized content
* placeholders
* formatting
* branding

Business logic never generates notification text.

---

## Provider Independent

Message delivery is delegated to Notification Providers.

Examples:

* Email
* SMS
* WhatsApp
* Push Notifications

Changing providers requires only configuration changes.

---

## Configurable

Each property controls:

* enabled channels
* notification timing
* templates
* branding
* sender information

---

```text
Business Event
       │
       ▼
Notification Platform
       │
       ▼
Notification Template
       │
       ▼
Channel Selection
       │
       ▼
Notification Provider
       │
       ▼
```

The Notification Platform coordinates delivery while Providers perform communication.

Every notification follows the same lifecycle.

```text

    │
    ▼
Queued
    │
    ▼
Prepared
    │
    ▼
Delivered
```

Alternative states include:

* Scheduled
* Failed
* Cancelled
* Expired
* Retried

# 8. Templates

Every notification is generated from a template.

Templates support:

* localization
* branding
* placeholders
* conditional content

Example placeholders:

* Guest Name
* Property Name
* Check-in Date
* Door PIN
* Payment Amount

Templates should remain independent of notification channels.

---

# 9. Localization

Notifications should be delivered in the recipient's preferred language.

Language resolution follows this order:

Guest Preference

↓

Reservation Language

↓

Property Default

↓

Organization Default

↓

Platform Default

---

# 10. Scheduling

Notifications may be sent immediately or at scheduled times.

Examples:

* immediately after reservation
* 24 hours before arrival
* 2 hours before check-in
* after payment
* after checkout

Scheduling is coordinated by the Workflow Engine.

---

# 11. Recipient Preferences

Recipients may configure notification preferences.

Examples:

Preferred channel

Quiet hours

Marketing opt-in

Language

The Notification Platform respects these preferences whenever possible.

---

# 12. Delivery Tracking

Every notification is tracked.

Information includes:

* recipient
* channel
* provider
* delivery status
* timestamps
* retry count

Delivery history should remain available for auditing.

---

# 13. Workflow Integration

The Notification Platform integrates directly with the Workflow Engine.

Example:

```text
Reservation Created
        │
        ▼
Workflow Started
        │
        ▼
Notification Created
        │
        ▼
Channel Selected
        │
        ▼
Delivered
```

Notifications are outcomes of workflows, not independent business processes.

---

# 14. AI Integration

The AI Platform may interact with notifications.

Examples:

* explain previous notifications
* summarize communication history
* generate personalized messages
* suggest communication timing

AI never bypasses notification permissions or workflows.

---

# 16. Design Rules

The Notification Platform follows these mandatory rules.

* Business logic never sends messages directly.
* Every notification originates from a business event.
* Templates are separated from business logic.
* Delivery is delegated to Notification Providers.
* Notifications respect recipient preferences.
* Every delivery is tracked.
* Every notification is auditable.
* Channels remain interchangeable.

---

# 17. Future Evolution

The Notification Platform is designed to become the centralized communication layer of Hospitality OS.

As new communication channels emerge, they should integrate through the Provider Framework without affecting business workflows.

The long-term objective is to provide reliable, personalized and fully automated communication throughout the entire guest journey while maintaining consistency across every module and platform component.
