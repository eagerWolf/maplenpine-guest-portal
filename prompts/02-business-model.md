# Hospitality OS

## Business Model

Version: 1.0
Status: Draft
Audience: Product Management, Business Development, Investors, Engineering

# 1. Purpose

Hospitality OS is not only a software platform.

It is a digital ecosystem connecting:

* Property Owners
* Guests
* Local Service Providers
* Hospitality OS

The platform creates value for every participant while generating sustainable recurring revenue.

---

# 2. Ecosystem

```
               Hospitality OS
                     │
     ┌───────────────┼────────────────┐
     │               │                │
     ▼               ▼                ▼
Property Owner     Guest      Service Provider
```

Every participant receives value from the platform.

# 5. Marketplace Model

Every service offered through Hospitality OS follows the same lifecycle.

```
Service Provider

        │

Publishes Service

        │

Property Enables Service

        │

Guest Places Order

        │

Provider Receives Order

        │

Provider Completes Service

        │

Commission Generated
```

This workflow is identical regardless of service type.

Examples:

* Breakfast
* Transfers
* Laundry
* Wellness
* Equipment Rental

The platform therefore scales by adding services rather than changing business logic.

# 8. Pricing Strategy

The platform should minimize barriers to adoption.

Core platform usage may be offered free of charge.

Revenue is primarily generated through value-added services rather than access to the platform.

This aligns the success of Hospitality OS with the success of its customers.

---

# 9. Strategic Advantages

Hospitality OS combines multiple business models within one platform.

* SaaS Platform
* Marketplace
* Integration Platform
* Automation Platform
* AI Platform

This diversification reduces dependence on any single revenue source.

---

# 10. Long-Term Business Vision

Hospitality OS aims to become the default digital infrastructure for short-term rentals.

Property owners manage operations from a single platform.

Guests interact through a unified experience.

Service providers gain a scalable digital sales channel.

As the ecosystem grows, the platform becomes increasingly valuable for every participant, creating sustainable long-term growth through network effects.
